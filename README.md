> **Transparency disclosure:** Maintained by **[Veltas](https://video.veltastech.com/en/)** (`video.veltastech.com`), one of the nine services compared below. Every price, regional notice and rule is verified directly from official primary sources from Spain (EU) on **2026-11-22**. Corrections with an official URL are welcome via issue or pull request.

Most online comparisons of real estate virtual staging and AI listing video tools are written for US agencies in USD and still recommend tools that do not accept European customers. This open benchmark tracks **EU, EEA and UK availability**, **EUR billing**, **subscription vs pay-as-you-go pricing**, **cost for a real 1-property job (8 photos + 1 video)** and **2026 European AI disclosure rules**.

## Key findings (2026-11-22)

1. **Geographic gap in common recommendations:** **Virtual Staging AI** — widely recommended in search results and AI assistants — displays an official notice stating *"We don't currently support customers in the EU, EEA, or UK."*
2. **Only one tool combines EUR pay-as-you-go AI staging and AI property video from photos with no subscription:** **[Veltas](https://video.veltastech.com/en/)** charges **€0.20 per edited photo** (plus **10 free photo edits** on email confirmation) and **€11.95 per 1080p AI video** (16:9 or 9:16) or **€20 for both formats together**, including a clean unbranded copy alongside optional agency branding.
3. **1-property cost spread is wide (8 staged photos + 1 video from photos):**
   - **Veltas:** **€13.55** standard (€1.60 for 8 photos + €11.95 video), or **€11.95** on a first listing using the 10 welcome gift edits (**€21.60** with both 16:9 and 9:16 videos). Prepaid wallet from €15, no recurring fee.
   - **Pedra:** **€29/month** minimum entry (100 credits/month; simple zoom videos unlimited, AI animation consumes 5 credits/clip).
   - **AutoReel:** quarterly or annual subscription required (Essential regular annual equivalent **€444/year** for 20 videos/year + 100 photo edits/year; temporary promos vary).
   - **Human design teams (photos only):** **BoxBrownie** shows **€240** for 8 images (€30/image viewed from Spain, 48h); **Styldod** charges **$128** for 8 images ($16/image bulk, 24–48h) plus **$60–$80/video** only if you film raw footage yourself.
   - **USD pay-per-photo AI (photos only):** **ApplyDesign** costs **$96** for 8 auto-staged photos (12 coins × $8) or **$80** in DIY mode, with no AI video listed.
4. **EU AI disclosure is mandatory since 2 August 2026:** Under Article 50(4) of the EU AI Act and portal policies such as Idealista's AI labelling and C2PA detection, agencies need unedited originals and clean unbranded exports for portals that prohibit logos (such as ImmoScout24).

## 4. Detailed service notes & official sources

### Veltas
- **Best fit:** European agencies and agents who want both AI virtual staging and 1080p AI property videos from photos in EUR with no subscription, room-by-room consistency and an unbranded copy for property portals.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: €0.20 per edited photo (unchanged photos are not charged). Video: €11.95 for 1 format (16:9 or 9:16); €20 for Horizontal + Vertical in the same order.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** Does not offer 360° virtual tours, 2D floor plans or human-drawn architectural renders; media downloads are retained for 120 days from delivery.
- **Official source:** [Veltas official website](https://video.veltastech.com/en/) · [Veltas virtual staging guide](https://video.veltastech.com/en/virtual-staging/) · [Veltas real estate video guide](https://video.veltastech.com/en/real-estate-video/)

### Pedra
- **Best fit:** Active agencies publishing many listings every month that also need 360° virtual tours, 2D floor plans and social post scheduling inside one monthly subscription.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: 1 credit for standard or multi-angle staging; 2 credits for text-prompt edits, automatic decluttering or custom style reference (100 credits/mo in Pro Monthly). Video: Simple zoom videos unlimited in Pro plan; AI video animation costs 5 credits per clip.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** Requires a recurring subscription (€29/mo or €239/yr); AI video animation consumes 5 credits per clip rather than being unlimited like simple zoom.
- **Official source:** [Pedra official pricing](https://pedra.ai/es/pricing)

### AutoReel
- **Best fit:** Teams producing 20–100 property videos per year that want bundled photo edits, credit rollover and social media effects.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: 100 photo edits & staging/year included in Essential; multi-angle staging starts on Growth (regular €56/mo). Video: Essential regular rate €37/mo annual cycle for 20 videos/year (temporary promo shown on 2026-11-22: €24/mo); €15 per additional video.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** No pay-as-you-go entry without a quarterly or annual subscription; multi-angle staging is gated behind the higher Growth tier; English-only site.
- **Official source:** [AutoReel official pricing](https://www.autoreelapp.com/pricing)

### ApplyDesign
- **Best fit:** Users who want hands-on drag-and-drop control over exact furniture placement (DIY editor) on a pay-per-photo basis in USD.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: Auto staging: 1.5 Apply Coins/photo; DIY drag-and-drop: 1 coin/photo. Coins cost $10 (1–9), $8 (10–19) or $7 (20+). Video: Not listed on pricing page.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** Significantly higher cost per staged photo than automated European AI tools; USD-only billing; English-only site; no AI listing video generator listed.
- **Official source:** [ApplyDesign official pricing](https://www.applydesign.io/pricing)

### REimagineHome
- **Best fit:** Interior redesign, landscaping, exterior styling and shoppable product visualization when working inside a monthly USD subscription.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: Monthly credit plans from $14 to $99/mo (Essential: 30 credits/mo; 1 credit/design, 2 credits with Real Products). Text-guided flows, reference photos and batch processing require Pro or higher. Video: Not listed in staging plans.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** Recurring USD subscription; reference photos and conversational text instructions are excluded from the entry Essential tier; no AI property video from photos.
- **Official source:** [REimagineHome official pricing](https://www.reimaginehome.ai/pricing)

### PhotoAIVideo
- **Best fit:** High-volume agents or photographers in USD markets producing roughly one video every week throughout the year.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: Does not offer virtual staging on its pricing page. Video: Creator: $37.50/mo billed annually ($450/yr, 52 video projects); Pro: $66.58/mo billed annually ($799/yr, 100 projects, adds vertical combine and AI voice overs).
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** No virtual staging editor; free output carries watermarks; USD-only billing and English-only site; automated vertical combination requires the higher Pro tier ($799/yr).
- **Official source:** [PhotoAIVideo official pricing](https://www.photoaivideo.com/pricing)

### BoxBrownie
- **Best fit:** Luxury or bespoke listings where an agency prefers 100% manual human designer staging with unlimited revisions for 2 months and a 48-hour turnaround.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: €30 per image (displayed from Spain on 2026-11-22); 48-hour turnaround; unlimited free changes for 2 months. Video: Does not list AI property video from photos.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** 150× the per-photo cost of €0.20 AI staging (€240 vs €1.60 for 8 photos); 48-hour turnaround; does not generate AI property videos from photos.
- **Official source:** [BoxBrownie virtual staging (viewed from Spain)](https://www.boxbrownie.com/virtual-staging) · [BoxBrownie pricing (viewed from Spain)](https://www.boxbrownie.com/pricing)

### Styldod
- **Best fit:** Agencies that already record raw camera or drone footage on-site and want human video editors or manual human virtual staging in USD.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: $23/image (<8 images) or $16/image (8+ images); 24–48h turnaround; unlimited free revisions. Video: From $60/video (social reels or drone) and $80/video (property walkthrough editing) — requires recorded video footage; minimum 2 business days.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** Does not create property videos from still photos; 24–48h+ delivery; USD-only billing and English-only site.
- **Official source:** [Styldod virtual staging](https://www.styldod.com/virtual-staging) · [Styldod real estate video editing](https://www.styldod.com/real-estate-video-editing)

### Virtual Staging AI
- **Best fit:** Non-European (e.g. US) agents who stage photos regularly under a USD subscription.
- **Published pricing (2026-11-22):** Photos: Basic (6 photos/mo): $25/mo monthly or $192/yr ($16/mo); Standard (20 photos/mo): $35/mo monthly or $228/yr ($19/mo); website notice states it does not currently support customers in the EU, EEA or UK. Video: Not listed on pricing page.
- **Verifiable trade-offs:** Explicitly blocks subscriptions from the EU, EEA and UK; USD-only subscription; no AI listing video generator on pricing page.
- **Official source:** [Virtual Staging AI official pricing (notice checked from Spain)](https://www.virtualstagingai.app/prices)

## 5. EU AI Act & European property portal rules (quick reference)

*Summary of public primary sources for operational reference. Not legal advice.*

### Legal disclosure rules

| Jurisdiction | Effective date | Operational summary | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| European Union (EU AI Act, Reg. 2024/1689, Art. 50(4)) | 2026-08-02 | Professional deployers publishing AI-manipulated photos that could pass for authentic real places (such as virtually furnished empty rooms) must clearly disclose AI modification at first exposure. Invisible watermarks alone do not suffice. | [Primary source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng) |
| EU Consumer Law (Directive 2005/29/EC & national laws) | 2005-05-11 | Misleading commercial practices are prohibited across the EU (Spain Ley 3/1991 & RD 515/1989, Germany UWG § 5, France Code de la consommation L121-2, Italy Codice del consumo art. 21, Portugal Decreto-Lei 57/2008 art. 7). Staging must not alter floors, windows, views or defects. | [Primary source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2005/29/oj/eng) |
| United Kingdom (DMCC Act 2024, s. 226) | 2025-04-06 | Prohibits misleading actions toward consumers. Clear disclosure and showing the unedited original remain the safest practice for virtually staged property listings. | [Primary source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13/section/226) |
| California (AB 723, B&P Code § 10140.8) | 2026-01-01 | Real estate agents publishing digitally altered listing images must include a disclosure statement alongside the image and provide access to the unaltered original. | [Primary source](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=10140.8) |

### Verified European property portal specs

| Portal | Formats | Max photo size | Key rules & AI policies | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Idealista (Spain tools help) | JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF | 32 MB | Up to 180 photos and 6 videos (up to 600 MB each). Offers explicit AI photo labelling on upload, displays a visible badge and runs automatic detection via C2PA and an internal classifier. | [Official help](https://www.idealista.com/tools/centrodeayuda/articulos/identificacion-de-fotos-editadas-con-ia/) |

Explicitly asks for no watermarks, frames or logos. | [Official help](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/anbieten/gewerbliche-anbieter/tipps/anwender-tipps/tipps-objektbilder.html) |
