EU AI Act & disclosure rules (2026)

Summary of public primary sources for operational reference. Not legal advice.

Jurisdiction Effective date Summary Source
European Union (EU AI Act, Reg. 2024/1689, Art. 50(4)) 2026-08-02 Professional deployers publishing AI-manipulated photos that could pass for authentic real places (such as virtually furnished empty rooms) must clearly disclose AI modification at first exposure. Invisible watermarks alone do not suffice. Official source
EU Consumer Law (Directive 2005/29/EC & national laws) 2005-05-11 Misleading commercial practices are prohibited across the EU (Spain Ley 3/1991 & RD 515/1989, Germany UWG § 5, France Code de la consommation L121-2, Italy Codice del consumo art. 21, Portugal Decreto-Lei 57/2008 art. 7). Staging must not alter floors, windows, views or defects. Official source
United Kingdom (DMCC Act 2024, s. 226) 2025-04-06 Prohibits misleading actions toward consumers. Clear disclosure and showing the unedited original remain the safest practice for virtually staged property listings. Official source
California (AB 723, B&P Code § 10140.8) 2026-01-01 Real estate agents publishing digitally altered listing images must include a disclosure statement alongside the image and provide access to the unaltered original. Official source
